## Priority 1: Structural Surgery

These changes address the core structural problems both editors identified.

### 1.1 Merge or Radically Shorten Chapters 4 & 5
- [ ] **Option A (recommended):** Combine into single chapter "Constants and Functions" — the distinction between numbers (pi, e) and functions (gamma, Bessel) doesn't warrant separate chapters
- [ ] **Option B:** Keep separate but cut each by 40% — five constants don't need 2,000 words
- [ ] Break the repetitive section-by-section structure (Pi → Primes → Random → Fibonacci → etc.)
- [ ] Add at least one extended close reading of a specific post per topic instead of just counts

### 1.2 Rewrite or Cut Chapter 6 (Mathematicians Behind the Theorems)
- [ ] Decide: reframe around the *relationship* question ("how does a modern mathematician position himself relative to giants?") or cut entirely
- [ ] If keeping: remove the arbitrary category labels (Giants / Intuitive Genius / Connector / etc.)
- [ ] Move Atiyah interview material to Chapter 10
- [ ] Reduce mention tallying; increase interpretation

### 1.3 Fix Chapter 10 (Interviews)
- [ ] Cut the catalog portions (listing subjects by date and word count)
- [ ] Focus on 2-3 interviews in depth: Atiyah, Ghrist, one entrepreneur
- [ ] Add analysis of *how* Cook listens, what he asks, what the interviews reveal about sustained practice

## Priority 6: Line-Level Patterns

Apply these patterns throughout the manuscript:

### 6.1 Sharpen Chapter Openings
- [ ] Each chapter opener should explicitly restate its guiding question in plain language
- [ ] Lead with what's at stake for the reader, not with background

**Example fix (Chapter 2):**
> Before: "WordPress requires categories; many bloggers treat this as a chore."
> After: "The blog's twenty-six categories are the closest thing Cook has to a table of contents for his own mind."

### 6.2 Fix Chapter Endings
- [ ] Audit all chapter endings — replace restatements with arrivals
- [ ] Chapter 1 ending too similar to prologue ending — find different landing

### 6.3 Trim Parenthetical Explanations

- [ ] Remove obligatory-feeling definitions
