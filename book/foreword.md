My freshman year of college, I was sitting in an honors ethics seminar at 8am on a Thursday. Douglas Weathers, a senior math major, walked in and made a beeline for me. “Last night I found out I follow your dad on Twitter! I love his stuff!” 

And that was when I realized that my dad’s blog was a bigger deal than any of us ever expected. 

That moment was in 2013, and the blog and its following have only grown since then. 

I mentioned offhandedly at one point that it would be funny to collect some stats on the stats blog, and here we are – a deep analysis of the first seventeen years of my dad’s blogging career. 

Through this exercise, I learned that way more people than Douglas Weathers care what my dad has to say in the world of statistics – some of these citations are highly impressive and I had no idea until we started looking them up. This compilation of anecdotes, equations, and general fun facts tied to statistics is a pillar of a niche community – as is the man who wrote it all. To me, John Cook is a humble, hardworking, loving father, but to this part of the Internet he is an eloquent, intellectual, creative savant. I’m grateful for the chance to understand more of this side of him, and look forward to putting more numbers to the scale of his success. 

Thank you, Dad, for supporting me through calculus, job hunts, and parenting. And thank you, reader, for supporting my dad through the notebook, the crucible, and the institution. Here’s to the next seventeen years of blogging. 

- Elizabeth Goode (née Cook)
