# Chapter 10: The Shape of Persistence

> "The limiting factor on statistics projects has rarely been my knowledge of statistics. The limiting factors are things like communication, endurance, organization."
> — John D. Cook

By 2025: 5,233 posts, 1.8 million words, unmeasured influence.

The full shape: persistence that made everything else possible.

## The Pandemic Invariance

March 2020 upended most patterns. Schools closed. Offices emptied. Conferences cancelled. World rhythms broke.

Blog rhythms did not.

| Year | Posts | Avg Words |
|------|-------|-----------|
| 2019 | 288 | 446 |
| 2020 | 288 | 451 |
| 2021 | 341 | 451 |

2020 indistinguishable from surrounding years. Twenty-two posts in March (the month everything changed), 21 in April, 23 in May. No spike of anxiety-posting. No drought of distraction. Just another year.

Striking flatness. The practice serves a deeper function: a place where external chaos gets ordered into mathematical logic.

Rarely commenting on the pandemic directly. A handful of posts use COVID-19 data as statistical examples. Otherwise, simply continuing. Another post about probability. Another about Python. Another about number theory.

The world outside changed. Function did not.

---

## No Gap Longer Than Eleven Days

Every year, every season, for nearly eighteen years.

Longest gap: July 9-21, 2014 (11 days).
Second-longest gaps: 8 days, appearing three times.

No gap reaches two weeks. Most professionals take longer vacations. A two-week holiday would set a record.

Not workaholism: posting frequency is measured, not manic. Something else: a commitment to showing up regardless of mood or circumstance.

Whatever else happened—travel, illness, family, career changes—the blog kept appearing.

---

## The Trade-Off That Didn't Hold

Early data suggested a trade-off: depth versus frequency. Fewer posts meant longer posts.

Later data broke the pattern:

| Era | Years | Posts | Avg Words | Total Words |
|-----|-------|-------|-----------|-------------|
| Early | 2008-2012 | 1,734 | 232 | 401,870 |
| Middle | 2013-2017 | 968 | 297 | 287,865 |
| Recent | 2018-2025 | 2,531 | 448 | 1,133,465 |

The recent era produced more posts AND longer posts. More total words than the first two eras combined. Assumed trade-off didn't hold.

Mastery. Early years learning the form. Middle years consolidation. Recent years: producing more, with more substance, without apparent strain.

## The Full Arc

From 2008 to 2025:

**2008-2012 (The Notebook):** 1,734 posts averaging 232 words. A consultant's scratchpad. Scattered topics. Short observations. Establishing the habit.

**2013-2017 (The Crucible):** 968 posts averaging 297 words. The quiet year. The career transition. The shedding of professional obligations. Finding the mathematical core.

**2018-2025 (The Institution):** 2,531 posts averaging 448 words. Focused. Deep explanations. Interconnected references. Infrastructure.

Trajectory not linear. Dipping in 2014. Transforming after 2016. But direction clear: from scattered to focused, from brief to substantial, from notebook to institution.

## For Anyone Starting

What sustained practice produces:

**Start before you're ready.** The 2008 blog wasn't polished. Exploratory. Polish came later.

**Let interests compete.** Early breadth wasn't a mistake. An experiment. Middle narrowing happened because the experiment produced a winner.

**Trust the process to clarify.** The 2014 dip wasn't failure. Focus emerging. Sometimes fewer posts means better posts.

**Keep showing up.** Later quality came from repetition. Explaining the gamma function and elliptic curves so many times that explanations became effortless.

---

## The Question

Proving the thesis: you can just do things.

Write about what interests you. Publish without permission. Show up day after day. Let the work find its form. No plan needed. No platform needed. Time and consistency.

Not just what Cook will do next, but what anyone can build with seventeen years of consistent effort.
