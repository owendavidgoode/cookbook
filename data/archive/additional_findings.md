## V. The First Blog Post

**Title:** "Moore's Law and Software Bloat"
**Date:** January 9, 2008
**URL:** https://www.johndcook.com/blog/2008/01/09/moores-law-and-software-bloat/

### Summary
Cook observed that when comparing a 1986 Mac to a 2007 PC:
- New machine dominates on number crunching
- Old Mac slightly wins on mundane tasks (boot, launch Word, search/replace)
- Conclusion: "Software bloat has increased at roughly the same rate as Moore's law"

### 2014 Update
> "Processors have not gotten much faster in the time since this post was first published, though we have more of them. Application startup times have improved—software companies have become more conscious of bloat precisely because the free lunch of faster processors is over."

**Significance:** This first post established themes Cook would return to repeatedly—the gap between theoretical capability and practical experience, the importance of measuring what actually matters.
