# Metastats Calendar Cover Spec

## Goal

Clean, minimalist cover image for a **365-day facts desk calendar / card deck**, not a book. It should feel like a data project: nerdy, precise, and calm, not loud or “marketing-y.”

## Text

- Main title (required): `Metastats`
- Subtitle (required): `365 Days of Facts about JohnDCook.com`
- No author name, no tagline, no extra copy.

## Composition & Layout

- Orientation: vertical, 4:5 ratio (e.g. 2400×3000 px), safe for cropping.
- Overall look: mostly negative space with a simple, abstract data motif.
- Layout:
  - Title centered horizontally in the upper third.
  - Subtitle directly under the title, smaller and lighter weight.
  - Abstract data/calendar graphic in the lower two thirds, but still airy.
- Keep the design **uncluttered**: no borders, no busy textures, no fake “book spine.”

## Visual Concept (Data + Calendar)

- Primary motif: a **grid of 365 small dots or squares**, representing days.
  - Arranged in a clean grid (for example, 7×53 or 10×37) with subtle variations.
  - One or a few points can be highlighted to suggest “interesting days.”
- Alternative acceptable motifs (same vibe, pick one):
  - A smooth line chart or histogram subtly integrated behind the text.
  - Concentric circles or rings of small marks, suggesting time/years of posts.
- The data element should be **abstract, not literal charts with axes or labels**.

## Style & Color

- Style: modern, flat, and typographic — “data viz meets MOMA postcard.”
- Background: mostly white or very light neutral (off‑white or very light gray).
- Palette: 1–2 accent colors only, for example:
  - Dark charcoal (#222222) for text.
  - Accent: deep teal or blue (for example, #006D7D or #0B5FFF) for data marks.
- Avoid:
  - Gradients heavier than a very soft, subtle one.
  - Stock-photo textures, fake paper, drop shadows, lens flares.

## Typography

- Title: strong, clean serif or geometric sans (for example, Source Serif or Source Sans–like).
  - Large, high contrast against the background.
- Subtitle: smaller sans-serif, medium weight, generous tracking.
- All caps or title case for “Metastats” is fine; subtitle in title case or sentence case.
- No script fonts, no novelty fonts.

## Use of Portrait (Optional)

- Prefer **no full-bleed portrait** on this cover.
- If a portrait is used at all, it should be:
  - Cropped small (for example, a circular thumbnail or tiny inset) and aligned near a corner.
  - Treated as a subtle accent, not the hero element.
- A fully abstract, portrait-free design is perfectly acceptable and preferred for a “clean” look.

## Generator Prompt (Short Version)

Minimalist vertical cover design, white background, central title “Metastats” with subtitle “365 Days of Facts about JohnDCook.com”, clean modern typography, abstract grid of 365 small dots suggesting a data-driven calendar, limited palette of charcoal and deep teal, lots of negative space, no extra text, flat vector style.

## Output Requirements

- Format: PNG.
- Resolution: at least 2400×3000 px (or higher), print-ready.
- Keep all text at least 5–8% in from each edge for print trimming.
